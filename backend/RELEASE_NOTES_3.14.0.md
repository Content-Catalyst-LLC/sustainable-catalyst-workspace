# Workspace v3.14.0 Release Notes

**Release:** Source Reliability, Provenance & Evidence Integrity Workspace

- Adds versioned source records with source/content fingerprints and retrieval/publication metadata.
- Adds explicit provenance events for acquisition, import, copy, transformation, extraction, verification, transfer, and related actions.
- Adds chain-of-custody event records with custodian, location, receipt, and fingerprint context.
- Adds human-authored integrity assertions such as hash-match, signature-valid, metadata-consistent, provenance-complete, and custody-intact.
- Adds source-to-claim/entity/event/document/testimony/media/spatial/scientific-object evidence bindings.
- Adds source-integrity graph, descriptive diagnostics, descriptive integrity assessment, and immutable snapshots.
- Integrates source integrity into Unified Research Context.
- Preserves v3.13 media provenance, v3.12 spatial evidence, v3.11 documentary evidence, v3.10 entity resolution, v3.9 timeline reconstruction, and Catalyst Analytics R 2.2.0.
- Adds migration 045.
- Does not automatically score reliability or credibility, determine authenticity, verify integrity, rank evidence, determine truth, infer culpability, or choose a preferred narrative.
