# Entity, Actor & Relationship Resolution Workspace v3.10.0

## Purpose
Represent people, organizations, institutions, agencies, companies, locations, accounts, documents, events, systems, aliases, identifiers, relationships, and possible duplicate identities as explicit reviewable research objects.

## Human-control boundary
Candidate matches are review objects, not merges. A confirmed match does not mutate or collapse either entity. `same-as` is a separate human-asserted relationship. Resolution diagnostics are descriptive only.

## Cross-layer integration
Entity graphs can overlay the v3.9 timeline reconstruction graph and link entities explicitly to statements, events, evidence references, document references, and scientific-object references. Unified Research Project Context exposes entity counts, relationship counts, context links, and descriptive resolution issues.
