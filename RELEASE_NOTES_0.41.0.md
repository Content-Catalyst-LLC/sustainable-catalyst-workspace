# Sustainable Catalyst Workspace v0.41.0 — Unified Research Navigation & Information Architecture

## Summary

v0.41.0 reorganizes Workspace around five primary areas: Start, Projects, Research, Review, and Exchange. It replaces the flat seventeen-destination navigation strip with contextual deep routes while preserving every existing surface and canonical record.

## Research information architecture

Research becomes the coherent home for Integrated Knowledge, Notebook, Personal Knowledge, and Graph. The Research home adds task-oriented pathways for finding material, working in notebooks, organizing structured knowledge, and inspecting explicit relationships. Source and Evidence remain canonical Workspace object types rather than becoming additional competing navigation destinations. Synthesis and Grounded Assistance remain Notebook capabilities, reachable through the Research area.

## Review and exchange

Activity, Lifecycle, History, Changes, Reconcile, Safety, and Audit are grouped under Review. Import & Interoperability, Collaboration, Institutional Handoff, and Share are grouped under Exchange.

## Data boundary

This is a schema-stable information-architecture release: storage remains 35, Project/Export remain 20.0, and Notebook Workspace/Export remain 8.0. Navigation is derived from existing surfaces; no canonical content is moved, duplicated, merged, or rewritten.

## Accessibility

Primary and contextual navigation both support keyboard traversal, focus-visible presentation, responsive layouts, forced-colors compatibility, and existing section-focus movement.
