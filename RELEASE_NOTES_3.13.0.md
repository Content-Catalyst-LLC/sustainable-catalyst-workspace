# Workspace v3.13.0 Release Notes

**Release:** Media Artifact, Image, Video & Derivative Provenance Workspace

- Adds versioned image/video/audio/media artifact records without storing canonical binary media in Workspace.
- Requires source fingerprints and recorded content hashes.
- Adds frame/timecode/region locators and explicit derivative/transformation lineage.
- Adds human-authored links to entities, events, documents, testimony, statements, evidence, spatial observations, and scientific objects.
- Adds media provenance graph, integrity projection, descriptive diagnostics, and immutable snapshots.
- Integrates the media layer into Unified Research Context.
- Preserves v3.12 spatial evidence, v3.11 documentary evidence, v3.10 entity resolution, v3.9 timeline reconstruction, and Catalyst Analytics R 2.2.0.
- Adds migration 044.
- Does not automatically determine authenticity, tampering, identity, truth, culpability, similarity, or a preferred narrative.
