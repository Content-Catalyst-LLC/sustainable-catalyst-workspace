# Investigation Graph, Contradiction & Competing Hypothesis Workspace v3.8.0

This layer projects v3.7 claims, evidence links, and statement relations into a reproducible investigation graph. Competing hypotheses are explicitly grouped by humans; the system reports relationship counts and contradiction clusters descriptively. It does not select a winner or infer truth.

Schemas: `sc-workspace-investigation-graph-contradiction-hypothesis-workspace/1.0`, `sc-workspace-investigation-graph/2.0`.
