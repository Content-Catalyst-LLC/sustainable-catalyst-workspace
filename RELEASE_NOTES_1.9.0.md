# Sustainable Catalyst Workspace v1.9.0

## Institutional Governance, Provenance & Audit Studio

Adds an institutional audit surface that derives reproducible dossiers from existing authoritative Workspace ledgers. Evidence scope, provenance relationships, decisions, review-room snapshots, reconciliation receipts, lifecycle history, citations, and institutional handoffs remain traceable to their canonical source subsystems. User-supplied attestations and portable audit packages support review without regulatory-certification claims, compliance scoring, automatic submission, or canonical mutation.

Storage 35, Project 20.0, and Export 20.0 remain frozen.
