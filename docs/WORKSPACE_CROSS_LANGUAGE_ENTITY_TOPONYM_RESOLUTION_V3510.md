# Workspace v3.51.0 — Cross-Language Entity & Toponym Resolution Workspace

## Purpose
Resolve people, organizations, places, geographic features, works, events, concepts and other research entities across languages and historical naming systems without destroying original-language evidence.

## Rules
1. Original-language name forms remain first-class evidence.
2. Translation/transliteration is a derived name form, never a replacement identity.
3. Historical names, exonyms and endonyms remain explicit.
4. Resolution may be `resolved`, `ambiguous`, `unresolved`, or `rejected`.
5. No automatic entity merge is permitted.
6. Every accepted resolution can retain source and decision lineage.
