# v1.0.0 General Availability

General Availability is a release-control transition, not a schema migration or feature-expansion event. The v0.84.0 readiness dossier is retained as prerequisite evidence. The GA record requires explicit operator identity, production URL, eight checks, and human attestation.

The certificate remains `hold` until every prerequisite is complete. Workspace does not infer GA status or mutate project data.
