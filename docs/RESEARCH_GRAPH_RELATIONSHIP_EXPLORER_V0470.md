# Workspace v0.47.0 — Research Graph & Relationship Explorer

The Research Graph is derived at runtime from recorded Workspace state. It extends the earlier object graph to Notebook records, explicit notebook links/backlinks, promotion lineage, synthesis selection, research questions and claims, citation-library origins, provenance, evidence, analysis and decisions.

## Boundaries

- No graph database.
- No semantic embeddings.
- No hidden relationship inference.
- No automatic AI or canonical mutation.
- Citation nodes retain pointers to browser-local reference records rather than copying canonical research.
- Every edge has a recorded relationship source.
