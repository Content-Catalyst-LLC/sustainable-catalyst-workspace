# Institutional Research Packages — v0.57.0

v0.57.0 adds deliberately scoped, frozen institutional research packages. A package can contain selected Integrated Knowledge records plus optional recorded provenance, related Citation Library references, Research Tasks, and Collaboration review context.

Package creation is an explicit disclosure action. The source project remains unchanged, the package never refreshes automatically, and Workspace does not upload, publish, or grant institutional access. A deterministic fingerprint makes later inspection of the frozen artifact possible.

The earlier v0.19 Catalyst Intelligence promotion/receipt workflow remains available as a product-specific compatibility path. Institutional Research Packages are broader handoff bundles and do not automatically import into Catalyst Intelligence or establish organization permissions.
