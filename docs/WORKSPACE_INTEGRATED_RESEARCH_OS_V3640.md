# Workspace v3.64.0 — Integrated Research OS

Research lifecycle:

`Projects → Sources → Analysis → Models → Agents → Governance → Visualizations → Decisions → Publication`

v3.64 binds existing specialist-runtime objects into one provenance-preserving research context. It tracks cross-stage lineage, evaluates descriptive readiness, creates bounded handoff plans, snapshots the integrated state, and produces reproducible Research OS packages.

It preserves v3.60 agentic workflow, v3.61 human governance, v3.62 specialist coordination, and v3.63 reproducible research packaging.

The OS layer cannot automatically execute models or agents, approve or bypass governance, publish, create external side effects, determine truth, rank evidence, choose narratives, or make decisions.
