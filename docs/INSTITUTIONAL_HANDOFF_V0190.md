# Institutional Handoff — v0.19.0

Institutional Handoff is the explicit promotion boundary from personal Sustainable Catalyst Workspace projects into Catalyst Intelligence.

## Promotion model

1. Select a local Workspace project.
2. Select the canonical objects appropriate for institutional promotion.
3. Review the explainable readiness checklist.
4. Confirm the copy, governance, and sharing acknowledgements.
5. Export a SHA-256 fingerprinted promotion package.
6. Transfer it deliberately to the receiving institutional system.
7. Optionally import a matching institutional receipt to update the local handoff ledger.

The personal Workspace project remains unchanged and independent. A receipt can update only the handoff record; it cannot rewrite research, evidence, analysis, decisions, documents, or other canonical project objects.

## Readiness checklist

Readiness is not a numeric score. Workspace surfaces inspectable conditions such as selected scope, provenance coverage, unresolved high-priority research questions, challenged assumptions or contested findings, decisions marked for evaluation/revisit, stale reproducibility records, and open collaboration review threads.

## Privacy-minimized package

Promotion packages exclude browser/device identity, account identity, local persistence metadata, connected-tool handoff/session state, recent tools, Activity Intelligence history, Responsible AI review history, and Collaboration Foundation history.

## Institutional boundary

Workspace does not implement organization tenancy or institutional permissions. Catalyst Intelligence remains responsible for institutional identity, roles, access controls, governance, audit, shared knowledge, connectors, and operational controls after a copy is accepted.
