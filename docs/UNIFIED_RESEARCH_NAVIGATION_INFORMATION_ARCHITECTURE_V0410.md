# Unified Research Navigation & Information Architecture — v0.41.0

Workspace had accumulated seventeen top-level destinations by v0.40.0. v0.41.0 reduces that competition without reducing capability.

## Primary areas

1. Start — orientation, quick starts, recent work, diagnostics.
2. Projects — project and canonical object work.
3. Research — Integrated Knowledge, Notebook, Personal Knowledge, and Graph.
4. Review — Activity, Lifecycle, History, Change Review, Reconciliation, Safety, and Audit.
5. Exchange — Import/Interoperability, Collaboration, Institutional Handoff, and Share.

## Canonical ownership

The navigation layer owns no research content. A Notebook block remains a Notebook block; a Source remains a Workspace Object; a Research claim remains in the Research Workspace. Integrated Knowledge continues to derive its index from those records. Navigation changes only how users reach the owning surface.

## Research task model

Research home exposes four task-oriented routes: Find, Work, Organize, and Connect. These map to Research home, Notebook, Knowledge, and Graph. Synthesis and Grounded Assistance remain inside Notebook because they operate on explicit Notebook selections and provenance.

## Stability

No storage or project migration is required. Existing deep route identifiers are retained for compatibility with internal handoffs, saved UI state, and regression coverage.
