# Workspace v2.33.0 — Cross-Product Research Handoff Fabric

Introduces provenance-preserving research handoffs across Sustainable Catalyst products. Handoffs pin canonical scientific objects by revision and fingerprint, preserve source/destination product and intent, and emit durable create/accept receipts. Destination acceptance is explicit; Workspace does not provide a generic destination mutation endpoint.

Migration 030 adds durable handoff and handoff-receipt tables.
