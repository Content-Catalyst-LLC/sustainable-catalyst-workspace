# Sustainable Catalyst Workspace v0.72.0 — Research Workflow Guidance & Empty-State Refinement

## Purpose

v0.72.0 improves orientation after First-Run Onboarding by helping users understand the next explicit research action when a surface is blank or a project is only partially developed.

## Research workflow guidance

Research Home and Project Research now show a contextual next-step panel derived from visible local project state. The advisory sequence covers orienting to a project, framing a question, gathering sources, extracting evidence, connecting claims, synthesizing Notebook material, composing stable research, and reviewing explicit next actions.

The guidance is not a readiness score and does not infer that work is correct or complete.

## Empty states

Empty research questions, reading queues, claims, notebooks, task lists, and Integrated Knowledge results now explain what can populate the surface and preserve the relevant governance boundary.

## Governance

No automatic project mutation, task creation, AI invocation, lifecycle advancement, completion marking, background navigation, telemetry, or behavioral profiling is introduced.

## Schema stability

- Storage: `35`
- Project: `sc-workspace-project/20.0`
- Project Export: `sc-workspace-project-export/20.0`
- Migration: **not required**
