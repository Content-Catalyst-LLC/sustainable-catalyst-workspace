# Sustainable Catalyst Workspace v3.7.0

## Claims, Evidence & Investigative Research Workspace

This release extends the verified v3.6.0 visual research line with a provenance-aware investigative workspace.

### Added

- Versioned claim, finding, hypothesis, and question records.
- Immutable evidence-to-statement links.
- Source fingerprint pinning for non-Workspace evidence references.
- Validation of Workspace-local scientific-object evidence references.
- Statement-to-statement contradiction and competing-hypothesis relationships.
- Investigative graph projection over the v3.6 visual research graph.
- Reproducible investigative workspace snapshots.
- Unified Research Project Context projection and counts.
- 12 typed API operations and corresponding WordPress proxy/runtime methods.
- Migration 038.

### Preserved

- Workspace v3.6 visual research and scene-graph functionality.
- Workspace v3.5 Catalyst Analytics R provider 2.1.0.
- Platform Core v3 reference-first integration.
- Read-only/non-root hardened R runtime.

### Safety and epistemic boundaries

No automatic truth determination, evidence ranking, claim scoring, hidden hypothesis preference, or decision authority is introduced.
